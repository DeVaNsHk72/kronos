# Kronos frontend — surface brief

**Scope:** the whole `kronos-main/student` app: shell, landing, agent thread, and all
student and faculty routes.

**Mode:** Operate governs the world (the agent shell and every console route). The
landing route alone is Persuade and is the one place the 3D hero lives.

**Audience and job:** a student ranking topics under exam pressure; a lecturer
assembling a paper they must defend. Same archive, different verbs.

**Pinned by the user, not negotiable:** Material Design 3 as the real system (tonal
palettes, elevation, state layers, shape scale) expressed as CSS custom properties
over the existing Tailwind v4 + Radix layer — no new component library. The agent IS
the app: one conversation shell, every route reachable as a rendered result surface.
One atmospheric WebGL hero on the landing only; the app stays flat and fast.

**Memorable moment:** the blueprint matrix filling in under the agent's answer — the
cells the answer used lighting as it writes, so the reasoning and the evidence are the
same object.

**Unresolved:** what happens after the hackathon; whether faculty print styles need a
second pass once real paper output is tested.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The exam blueprint made live — the unit x marks x CO matrix both users
already argue over, with the agent writing into its cells. Refuses the category
arrangement: gray thread rail, centred bubble column, evidence demoted to cards
under the answer.

OWN-WORLD: Cyanotype, drenched. Prussian-blue field owns the viewport (MD3 tonal
palette seeded #12395E), drafting lines in white, one correction-red reserved for a
mark, a citation, or a failed constraint. Archivo alone from 11px to 96px, width axis
carrying register; Martian Mono only where text is literally machine output. Ruled
cells, never floating cards. Printable artifacts invert to white stock.

STORY: The visitor sees the archive is a structure, not a search box; believes every
number came from a real paper; asks in plain words and reads the cells that answered.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed blueprint field. A WebGL cyanotype grid resolving from
exposure into a real marks matrix, nine years of one subject. Wordmark at top-left in
drafting caps; the composer sits low-centre on the grid, white rule, one red caret.

FORM: The Blueprint — candidate 1 of my ordered list, taken by the user over assigned
candidate 4 (The Answer Sheet). Seed key b9ebe596.

RAISES (carried from declined challengers): fixed tabular slots for every quantity
(nixie); named agent states with a misfold printed on the surface, not a toast
(miura); one face across an extreme scale range (alphabet-storm).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
